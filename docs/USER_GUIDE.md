## Getting Started

### Creating Your Account

1. **Navigate to the Application**
   - Open your web browser
   - Go to the onboarding portal URL provided by HR
   - You should see the login page

2. **Register a New Account**
   - Click on the **"Register"** tab
   - Fill in the following information:
     - **Email**: Your official Myntra email address
     - **Password**: Create a strong password (minimum 8 characters)
     - **First Name**: Your first name
     - **Last Name**: Your last name
     - **Department**: Select your department from the dropdown
     - **Designation**: Your job title
     - **Joining Date**: Your official start date
   - Click **"Register"** button
   - You'll receive a confirmation message and be automatically logged in

3. **First Login**
   - If you already have an account, click the **"Login"** tab
   - Enter your email and password
   - Click **"Login"** button
   - You'll be redirected to your dashboard

### Understanding the Dashboard

Your dashboard is personalized based on your role:

**New Hire Dashboard** shows:
- Overall onboarding progress (percentage)
- Pending tasks with due dates
- Documents to upload and their verification status
- Training modules to complete
- Quick stats (tasks, documents, training)

**HR Admin Dashboard** shows:
- Overview of all new hires
- Pending document verifications
- System-wide statistics
- Recent hires and their progress

**Manager Dashboard** shows:
- Team members' onboarding progress
- Department statistics
- Pending team tasks

## For New Hires

### Step 1: Complete Your Profile

1. **Navigate to Profile**
   - Click on your name in the top-right corner
   - Select **"Profile"** from the dropdown

2. **Add Personal Information**
   - **Phone Number**: Enter your contact number
   - **Address**: Fill in your current address
   - **Emergency Contact**:
     - Name of emergency contact
     - Relationship (spouse, parent, sibling, etc.)
     - Emergency contact phone number
   - Click **"Save Profile"**

3. **Upload Profile Picture** (Optional)
   - Click on the profile picture placeholder
   - Select an image file (JPG, PNG)
   - Click **"Upload"**

### Step 2: Complete Your Tasks

1. **View Tasks**
   - Go to the **"Tasks"** section from the main menu
   - You'll see a list of all assigned tasks categorized by:
     - Pre-Joining
     - Day 1
     - Week 1
     - Month 1
     - Training
     - Documentation
     - IT Setup
     - HR Formalities

2. **Task Information**
   Each task shows:
   - Title and description
   - Category and priority (Low, Medium, High, Critical)
   - Due date
   - Current status (Pending, In Progress, Completed, Blocked)
   - Assigned by (HR or Manager)

3. **Update Task Status**
   - Click on a task to view details
   - Update the status:
     - **Pending**: Not started
     - **In Progress**: Currently working on it
     - **Completed**: Finished
     - **Blocked**: Cannot proceed (requires help)
   - Add notes if needed
   - Click **"Update Status"**

4. **Mark Task as Complete**
   - Once you've finished a task
   - Change status to **"Completed"**
   - Add completion notes
   - Your onboarding progress will automatically update

### Step 3: Upload Documents

1. **Navigate to Documents**
   - Click **"Documents"** in the main menu
   - You'll see all required and optional documents

2. **Upload a Document**
   - Click **"Upload Document"** button
   - Select document type from dropdown:
     - Aadhaar Card
     - PAN Card
     - Passport
     - Educational Certificates
     - Experience Letters
     - Address Proof
     - Bank Details
     - Profile Photo
     - Other
   - Click **"Choose File"** and select your document
   - File requirements:
     - Formats: PDF, JPG, PNG, DOC, DOCX
     - Maximum size: 5MB
   - Click **"Upload"**

3. **Check Document Status**
   - **Pending**: Waiting for HR review
   - **Verified**: Approved by HR
   - **Rejected**: Not accepted (check rejection reason)

4. **Re-upload if Rejected**
   - Read the rejection reason
   - Fix the issue (e.g., better quality image, correct document)
   - Delete the rejected document
   - Upload a new version

### Step 4: Complete Training Modules

1. **Access Training**
   - Click **"Training"** in the main menu
   - You'll see all available training modules

2. **Start a Training Module**
   - Click on a module to open it
   - Read the content carefully
   - Watch any videos if provided
   - Download resources if available
   - Mark your progress as you go

3. **Take the Quiz**
   - After completing the content, take the assessment quiz
   - Answer all questions
   - You need 70% or higher to pass (unless specified otherwise)
   - You can retake the quiz if you don't pass

4. **View Training Progress**
   - See your completion percentage
   - Check your quiz scores
   - View completed and pending modules

### Step 5: Monitor Your Progress

1. **Track Overall Progress**
   - Your dashboard shows overall onboarding completion percentage
   - This updates automatically as you complete tasks, upload documents, and finish training

2. **View Onboarding Status**
   - **Pending**: Not started (0%)
   - **In Progress**: Working on it (1-99%)
   - **Completed**: Finished (100%)

3. **Check Upcoming Deadlines**
   - Dashboard shows tasks with approaching due dates
   - Prioritize high-priority and urgent tasks

### Contacting Your Manager or Buddy

1. **View Your Manager**
   - Your manager is shown on your profile
   - You can see their name and email

2. **View Your Buddy Mentor**
   - Your buddy is assigned to help you during onboarding
   - Contact them for questions and guidance

3. **Communication**
   - Use the provided email addresses
   - Or use your company's communication tools (Slack, Teams, etc.)

## For HR Administrators

### Managing New Hires

1. **View All New Hires**
   - Navigate to **"Dashboard"** or **"Employees"**
   - See a list of all employees in the onboarding process
   - Filter by department, status, or joining date

2. **View Employee Details**
   - Click on an employee's name
   - See their complete profile, progress, tasks, and documents

3. **Assign Manager and Buddy**
   - Open employee profile
   - Click **"Assign Manager"** or **"Assign Buddy"**
   - Select from the dropdown list of eligible users
   - Click **"Save"**

### Creating and Assigning Tasks

1. **Create New Task**
   - Go to **"Tasks"** section
   - Click **"Create New Task"** button
   - Fill in task details:
     - **Title**: Clear, concise title
     - **Description**: Detailed instructions
     - **Category**: Select appropriate category
     - **Priority**: Low, Medium, High, or Critical
     - **Assign To**: Select employee(s)
     - **Due Date**: Set realistic deadline
     - **Dependencies**: (Optional) Select prerequisite tasks
   - Click **"Create Task"**

2. **Edit Existing Task**
   - Find the task in the task list
   - Click **"Edit"**
   - Make necessary changes
   - Click **"Save"**

3. **Delete Task**
   - Find the task
   - Click **"Delete"**
   - Confirm deletion (this action cannot be undone)

### Verifying Documents

1. **View Pending Documents**
   - Navigate to **"Documents"** section
   - Filter by status: **"Pending"**
   - You'll see all documents awaiting verification

2. **Review a Document**
   - Click on the document to view it
   - Check for:
     - Clarity and readability
     - Correct document type
     - Valid information
     - Proper format

3. **Verify Document**
   - If document is acceptable:
     - Click **"Verify"** button
     - Document status changes to **"Verified"**
     - Employee receives notification

4. **Reject Document**
   - If document is not acceptable:
     - Click **"Reject"** button
     - Enter rejection reason (be specific and helpful)
     - Example: "Image is too blurry. Please upload a clearer scan."
     - Click **"Submit"**
     - Employee receives notification with reason

### Creating Training Modules

1. **Create New Training Module**
   - Go to **"Training"** section
   - Click **"Create Module"** button
   - Fill in module details:
     - **Title**: Module name
     - **Description**: Brief overview
     - **Category**: Company Culture, Product Knowledge, Compliance, Technical, Soft Skills, Department-specific
     - **Content**: Full training content (supports rich text)
     - **Duration**: Estimated time in minutes
     - **Video URL**: (Optional) Link to training video
     - **Presentation URL**: (Optional) Link to slides
     - **Resources**: Add downloadable resources
     - **Is Required**: Mark if mandatory
     - **Order**: Display order number

2. **Add Quiz Questions**
   - In the training module form
   - Add quiz questions:
     - **Question**: Type your question
     - **Option A, B, C, D**: Type answer options
     - **Correct Answer**: Select the correct option (0-3)
   - Add multiple questions as needed
   - Set **Passing Score** (default 70%)

3. **Publish Module**
   - Review all information
   - Click **"Create Module"**
   - Module becomes available to all users

4. **Edit or Delete Module**
   - Find module in training list
   - Click **"Edit"** to modify
   - Click **"Delete"** to remove (careful: affects all users)

## FAQs

### General Questions

**Q: I forgot my password. How do I reset it?**
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page, enter your email, and follow the instructions sent to your email.

**Q: How long does onboarding typically take?**
A: On average, employees complete onboarding within 2-4 weeks, depending on the role and department.

**Q: Can I access the system from my mobile device?**
A: Yes, the application is mobile-responsive and works on smartphones and tablets.

**Q: Who do I contact if I have technical issues?**
A: Contact IT support at it-support@myntra.com or submit a helpdesk ticket.

### Task-Related Questions

**Q: What if I can't complete a task by the due date?**
A: Mark the task as "Blocked" and add a note explaining why. Your manager or HR will reach out to help.

**Q: Can I skip a task that doesn't apply to me?**
A: No, but you can contact HR or your manager to have the task removed from your list if it's truly not applicable.

**Q: Who assigns tasks to me?**
A: HR admins assign general onboarding tasks. Your manager may assign department-specific tasks.

### Document Questions

**Q: What if I don't have all required documents yet?**
A: Upload what you have and notify HR about any missing documents. They can grant extensions if needed.

**Q: Why was my document rejected?**
A: Check the rejection reason provided by HR. Common reasons include poor image quality, wrong document type, or unclear information.

**Q: Can I upload a document in a different language?**
A: Documents should be in English or have an official translation. Contact HR if you need guidance.

**Q: How long does document verification take?**
A: Typically 1-3 business days. If it's been longer, follow up with HR.

### Training Questions

**Q: Can I retake a quiz if I fail?**
A: Yes, you can retake quizzes multiple times. Try to review the material before retaking.

**Q: How many times can I attempt a quiz?**
A: There's no limit on quiz attempts, but aim to learn the material rather than guessing repeatedly.

**Q: Is training mandatory?**
A: Modules marked as "Required" must be completed. Optional modules are for additional learning.

**Q: Can I complete training modules in any order?**
A: Yes, unless there are specific dependencies noted in the module description.

## Troubleshooting

### Login Issues

**Problem**: "Invalid credentials" error
**Solution**:
- Double-check your email and password (case-sensitive)
- Try resetting your password
- Clear browser cache and cookies
- Contact IT support if issue persists

**Problem**: Account locked after multiple failed login attempts
**Solution**:
- Wait 15 minutes before trying again
- Or contact IT support to unlock your account immediately

### Page Loading Issues

**Problem**: Page not loading or displaying incorrectly
**Solution**:
- Refresh the page (F5 or Ctrl+R)
- Clear browser cache and cookies
- Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- Check your internet connection
- Try accessing from a different network

### File Upload Issues

**Problem**: "File upload failed" error
**Solution**:
- Check file size (must be under 5MB)
- Verify file format (PDF, JPG, PNG, DOC, DOCX only)
- Ensure stable internet connection
- Try compressing large files
- Try a different file or browser

**Problem**: Uploaded file not showing
**Solution**:
- Refresh the documents page
- Check if upload actually completed (you should see success message)
- Re-upload if necessary

### Data Not Updating

**Problem**: Progress percentage not updating
**Solution**:
- Allow a few minutes for system to recalculate
- Refresh the page
- Log out and log back in
- Contact support if still not updating after 1 hour
